# WPE-M7 visible ranges

A search result can highlight its visible text after opening the destination. Kobalt sends two fingerprint-bound source positions and the confirmed frame revision to the renderer. The retained view resolves the DOM range and returns its clipped rectangles. The host validates the bounds and ignores stale results. The query publishes no pixels and does not move the reading position.

The native strict-main-thread WPE reader fixture opened a match in a later spine item and verified that its resolved geometry was nonempty and inside the published viewport. The native and FW4 renderers built, the retained source-location fixture passed, and the Qt5 and Qt6 syntax and source checks passed.

The geometry query covers the active spine item. Saved highlights and multiword interaction have separate storage and gesture requirements.
